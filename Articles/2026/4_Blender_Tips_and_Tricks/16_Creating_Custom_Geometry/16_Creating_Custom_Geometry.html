~16 Creating Custom Geometry~

By StarsInDust

12/14/2026

 

Extruding From an Edge

We can create Custom Geometry to fill unique areas of our mesh. Here we are selecting just two vertices and extruding them, Extrude (E). Then you can use Scale (S), Rotate (R), and Move (G) to shape the new face so it fits the area you need. Then hit the F key on the keyboard, and fill that new piece of topology. So, now you have created a brand-new custom face.

 

We can create the second face by selecting the two vertices at the side of this new face hitting the E key on the keyboard and extruding it again.

 

Watch It- Remember to keep checking things from different angles to make sure you are fitting the face correctly.

 

Here we continue to build what we need.

 

Extruding from a Vertex

We can also extrude (E) from a single vertex. Hit (F) to fill with a face.
