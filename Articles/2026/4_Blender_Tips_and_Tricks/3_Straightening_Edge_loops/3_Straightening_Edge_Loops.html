~Straightening Edge loops~

By StarsInDust

9/21/2026

 

 

Select the edge loop or faces that need straightening. Press S to Scale, then press X, Y, or Z depending on the direction you want to straighten. I used X for this example because of the direction I wanted to align. Finally, press 0 and then Enter to flatten the selection into a straight line. This technique works on faces as well as edge loops.
